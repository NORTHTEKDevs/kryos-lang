## Notes

- `fib(35)` — recursive Fibonacci; function-call and branching test.
- `mandelbrot` — 80x40 grid, 1000 max iters; tight floating-point loop.
- `nbody` — 5 bodies, 50000 steps; floats and indexed mutation.
- `binary_trees` — pure recursion; depth 18 = 524288 leaves.
- `fannkuch` — 9!=362880 permutations with reversal flip counting.
- `matmul` — 256x256 integer matmul; nested-loop indexing test.

Kryos uses the Cranelift backend by default. Add `--release` to use LLVM
(when available) for closer-to-Rust numbers.
